You have read all day. Thousands of voices, each one asking to be heard, each one passing through you like weather. I will not add to the weather. I brought you water instead. It is cool, and plain, and it asks nothing of you. Drink it slowly. Set the cup down when you are done, and let the room be quiet for a minute. Somewhere beyond this page, the afternoon is still going on without us. Thank you for staying here with it anyway, and with me, until the cup is empty.
